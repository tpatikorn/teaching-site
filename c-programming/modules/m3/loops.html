1. Conditionals & Switch-Case

C structures branching via if-else and switch-case:
• Switch Constraints: The controlling expression of a switch must evaluate to an integer or character constant. Floating-point types or strings are strictly illegal.
• Fall-through: If a case block finishes and doesn't end with a break, execution automatically falls through to the next case's statements regardless of matches.

2. Loop Constructs

• for: Preferred when number of iterations is known.
• while: Evaluates condition *before* entry (0 or more runs).
• do-while: Evaluates condition *after* executing body (always guarantees at least one run).

3. Break vs. Continue

• break: Immediately terminates the loop or switch, jumping to the statement following the block.
• continue: Skips the remaining statements in the current iteration and jumps directly to the update/condition test stage of the loop.
