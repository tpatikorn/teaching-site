7.1 Recursion & Call Stacks

Trace push/pop activations on the call stack step-by-step using recursive factorial.

Concept Overview

Recursion is a method of solving problems where the solution depends on solutions to smaller instances of the same problem. Every recursive call pushes a new Activation Frame onto the system call stack. When the **Base Case** is reached, the frames begin popping off as they return values.
